---
name: Fulfillment lead time
description: Why cart-item notice and daily branch preparation must be treated separately when generating order slots.
---

Cart-item lead time is advance notice measured from when the order is placed. It must not be added to the opening hour of every requested future date. Branch-wide preparation and delivery offsets remain a separate daily opening floor under the current schedule policy.

**Why:** A product requiring 24 hours of notice pushed every future day's first slot beyond that same day's closing time, so shoppers could never select a slot even though the branches were open. Applying the item's notice to the order time instead allows later dates without weakening same-day notice.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when changing slot generation or order preview. Check a cart with a long-lead item on both an ineligible near date and an eligible later date. If branch-wide preparation itself can be longer than a business day, revisit the daily opening-floor policy rather than silently treating it as an item lead time.
